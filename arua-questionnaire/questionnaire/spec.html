Shown after the 12 common steps when user selects Erectile dysfunction. Steps in order: SHIM intro → SHIM Q1–5 → Context → Treatment → Safety → Lifestyle → Consent → Review → Complete.

SHIM assessment (Sexual Health Inventory for Men)

ed-shim-intro SHIM introduction Sexual health

"The next five questions are part of a standard clinical assessment for ED." Explains SHIM, timeframe (past 6 months), asks for honesty. Informational only — no answers required, Continue advances.

ed-shim-1 Q1: How do you rate your confidence that you could get and keep an erection? Q1 of 5
1 — Very low2 — Low3 — Moderate4 — High5 — Very high
Scale input (1–5). Score contributes to SHIM total (max 25). SHIM 1–7 = severe ED; 8–11 = moderate; 12–16 = mild-moderate; 17–21 = mild; 22–25 = no ED.
ed-shim-2 Q2: When you had erections with sexual stimulation, how often were your erections hard enough for penetration? Q2 of 5
0 — No sexual activity1 — Almost never/never2 — A few times (<half)3 — Sometimes (~half)4 — Most times (>half)5 — Almost always/always
ed-shim-3 Q3: During intercourse, how often were you able to maintain your erection after penetration? Q3 of 5
0 — Did not attempt1 — Almost never2 — A few times3 — Sometimes4 — Most times5 — Almost always
ed-shim-4 Q4: During intercourse, how difficult was it to maintain your erection to completion? Q4 of 5
0 — Did not attempt1 — Extremely difficult2 — Very difficult3 — Difficult4 — Slightly difficult5 — Not difficult
ed-shim-5 Q5: When you attempted intercourse, how often was it satisfactory for you? Q5 of 5
0 — Did not attempt1 — Almost never2 — A few times3 — Sometimes4 — Most times5 — Almost always

Consent (ED)

ed-consent Just a few things to confirm. Before you submit

All five statements must be checked to proceed. Risk lane is computed when this step is passed.

I understand this is a telehealth service — assessment and prescription issued remotely by a NZ-registered clinician. I consent to my health information being stored and used in accordance with the NZ Privacy Act 2020. I understand a prescription is not guaranteed — clinician may recommend video check-in or decline to prescribe. The information I've provided is accurate and complete. I am 18 years of age or older and a resident of New Zealand.
