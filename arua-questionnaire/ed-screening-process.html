ED Screening — Process Overview

Pre-launch v1  ·  Confidential  ·  June 2026

Purpose

This document describes the pre-launch ED suitability screening flow. Its purpose is to quickly identify whether a prospective patient is likely suitable for Arua's online ED treatment pathway, and to capture their contact details so the team can reach out when the service launches.

This is not a full clinical intake. Hard contraindications are screened out immediately. Everyone else who completes the form is captured as a lead, with any clinical cautions flagged for review when the time comes.

Process flow

1
Entry

Prospective patient lands on the screening page via a CTA link. No account created, no data stored yet.

2
Suitability screen — 12 questions

Short yes / no / not sure questions covering eligibility, safety, medicines, and health history. Each answer routes to one of four outcomes (see below). Questions advance automatically on selection.

3a
S0 — Urgent redirect

User reports emergency symptoms. Full-screen stop. Directed to call 111. No data captured.

3b
S1 — Hard stop

User has a clear contraindication (nitrates, poppers, riociguat, under 18, outside NZ, recent major cardiac event, NAION, sex medically inadvisable). Full-screen stop. Directed to GP or urgent care. No data captured.

3c
S2 / S3 — Contact capture

User passes or has caution flags. Presented with a contact form: first name, last name, date of birth, email, NZ mobile. On submit, data is sent to Airtable. S2 submissions include a plain-English list of which questions raised a caution.

4
Thank you screen

User is told they're on the list and that Arua will be in touch when ready to launch. No clinical commitments are made at this stage.

Outcome codes

S0 — Urgent redirect
Emergency symptoms present

Pathway stops immediately. User directed to call 111. No data captured.

S1 — Hard stop
Clear contraindication or ineligible

Pathway stops. User directed to GP or urgent care. No data captured.

S2 — Needs review
Caution flag present

User continues to contact form. Specific flags recorded in Airtable for clinical review when contacted.

S3 — Clear
Likely suitable

User continues to contact form. No flags recorded. Standard outreach when service launches.
