About us

It started with three mates talking.

Three of us who talk about almost everything: work, kids, sport, money. It took a health scare to add health to the list. Arua is what came out of that conversation.

The team

Three mates. One standard.

Between us: six kids, a few dodgy knees, and careers in medicine, supply chains and big brands, mostly offshore, before we came home to raise our families. We know exactly how long blokes leave things, because we've done it too. Arua is that experience pointed at a problem worth fixing: making it easier for New Zealand men to sort their health.

Matt

Founder

Matt is the science guy. A doctor by training, he moved from medicine into consulting and pharma startups, then home to the Bay of Plenty. If Arua offers it, Matt has read the evidence behind it. If the evidence isn't there, we don't offer it. Dad of two, happiest on the water.

Matt@arua.co.nz

The standard

Clinical decisions sit with clinicians.

We build the pathway: the assessment, the delivery, the follow-up. What we don't do is prescribe. Every clinical decision is made by a NZ-registered clinician, based on your case and nothing else.

If treatment isn't appropriate, you'll be told plainly, and pointed in the right direction. That's the standard everything above is built around.

NZ-registered clinicians

All prescribing done by licensed NZ GPs and nurse practitioners.

Medsafe-approved medication

Evidence-based, approved treatments only. Generic where available.

Plain packaging always

No Arua branding on the outside. Your privacy is non-negotiable.

Privacy Act 2020 compliant

Health information handled as sensitive information under New Zealand law.
