How it works

Four steps. No waiting rooms. Everything handled.

01

Answer a few questions

Tell us what you're experiencing. Takes about 5 minutes. Private and confidential.

02

A clinician reviews your case

A NZ-registered clinician reviews your answers, talks it through with you by video, and determines what's appropriate for you.

03

Treatment dispatched

Your prescription is fulfilled within 48 hours: plain packaging to your door, or pickup from a pharmacy near you. Your choice.

We stay with you

Arua manages your repeat prescriptions, check-ins, and adjustments. A relationship, not a transaction.
